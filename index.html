03Method

Low-risk to start. Predictable to scale.

  1. 1

    Phase one · 1–2 weeks

    Discovery

    A short, fixed-fee engagement. We map the problem, the risks and the architecture, and give you a plan and estimate you can take to any vendor.

  2. 2

    Phase two · 2-week sprints

    Build

    Working software every sprint, demoed live in your time zone. Shared backlog, your tools, transparent burn against budget.

  3. 3

    Phase three · Ongoing

    Launch & scale

    Production rollout, monitoring and SLAs. Then either we keep evolving the product, or we hand over cleanly to your team, with docs.

Every engagement is led by an engineer with 8+ years of experience. No bait-and-switch after the sales call.
Written communication
Decisions, trade-offs and status are written down. Async-first, so time zones help rather than hurt.
Security as baseline
Least-privilege access, client-managed credentials, encrypted devices and offboarding checklists on every project.

05Questions

What buyers usually ask.

Something else? sales@winapps.co.in

Who owns the code and IP?

You do. Our contracts assign all intellectual property to you, and code is committed to repositories you control from the first day of the engagement.

How do you handle GDPR and data protection?

We sign a Data Processing Agreement on request, work in your environments with access you grant and revoke, and avoid copying production personal data to local machines. We're happy to complete your vendor security questionnaire.

How does the time difference work in practice?

Teams shift their day to keep at least 4 hours of overlap with Central European time and 3–4 hours with US Eastern. Standups, reviews and demos happen live; the rest is async, so work often moves forward while you sleep.

Can we interview the engineers?

Yes, and we encourage it. You see profiles, run your own technical interview and approve every person before they join. If someone isn't the right fit, we replace them at no cost.

How do contracts and payments work?

We invoice monthly in USD, EUR or GBP. We can work under your MSA or ours. Discovery is fixed-fee; projects are milestone-based; pods are monthly with 30 days' notice.

How quickly can you start?

An intro call can usually happen within the week. Discovery typically starts within 1–2 weeks, and a full pod within 2–4 weeks of signing.
